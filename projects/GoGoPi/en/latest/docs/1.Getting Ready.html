1.1 GoGoPi Introduction ​

1.1.1 Introduction ​

The intelligent visual car GoGoPi is powered by Raspberry Pi. Equipped with 2 degrees of freedom pan-tilt and HD camera, GoGoPi coordinating with OpenCV can perform color tracking, facial tracking, finger recognition, etc.

Equipped with RGB glowing ultrasonic sensor, GoGoPi can control light color and perform obstacle avoidance. In addition, various sensors can be installed on GoGoPi for more perception functions.

For better experience, we have prepared abundant tutorials, software and open source game programs. You can follow the tutorial to experience the GoGoPi.

1.1.3 Usage Guidelines ​

Please observe the following precautions when using and storing this product:

  • Age Restriction: This product contains small parts and sharp pins, making it unsuitable for children under 12 years old.

  • Adult Supervision: Minors must use this product under the supervision and guidance of an adult.

  • Safety Warning: The product includes small and sharp components. Do not swallow or press them to avoid injury.

  • Electrical Safety: This product contains conductive components. Do not touch them with metal objects while powered on.

  • Servo Handling: Avoid forcibly twisting the servo after it is powered on to prevent damage.

  • Battery Storage: If the product will not be used for an extended period, fully charge the battery, remove it, and store it in a cool, dry place.

1.3 First Power-On ​

1.3.1 Charging the Car ​

Due to the battery not being fully charged during transportation for safety, it is recommended to fully charge the battery before the first use. The detailed charging method is as follows:

  1. Take out the battery charger, and install two 18650 batteries (optional purchase) into it. The positive and negative terminals must be matched correctly to avoid short circuits or damage to the device.Please do not reverse the polarity of the battery! Then connect the battery charger (optional purchase) to the charging head (5V 3A, needed to be prepared by yourself) with the data cable. Finally, connect the power supply.

  2. Wait for the charging to complete. The indicator of the adapter is green when it is not powered on, and it is red when it is powered on, indicating that it is charging. The charging time is about 5 hours, and the indicator changes from red to green to indicate that the charging is completed. After the charging is completed, please unplug the charger as soon as possible and do not keep charging.

  1. Please switch the battery box to “OFF” before installing the batteries.
  1. After the charging is completed, insert the batteries into the battery box, with positive to positive and negative to negative.
  1. Connect the power cable of the battery box to the DC port on the expansion board and turn on the switch of the battery box. “OFF” is for turning off, and “ON” is for turning on.

NOTE

Use instructions for lithium batteries:

1. Please use available specialized charger. When the battery charger is connected to the battery without being plugged into an AC power outlet, the charger LED indicator will turn green.

2. When the charger is connected to battery and AC power outlet, the red indicator means that the battery is being charged. When the red indicator light turns green, the battery is fully charged. After charging, remove the charging cable and switch timely to prevent battery from damage caused by overcharging.

3. If the battery butted line is not connected to the controller, please do not directly connect to the battery to prevent short circuit caused by the contact of the positive and negative poles.

4. Connect the battery butted line into the Raspberry Pi expansion board first before using GoGoPi. Red line to red end and black to black end.

5. If the robot is not used for a long time, please fully charge the robot and then disconnect the line between connecting the robot and the battery. Do not fiercely pull the line to prevent the interface from damage.

6. Keep the battery far away from high temperature and all kinds of liquids to prevent overheating, fire or damp, which results in function decline and decreased service life

1.3.2 Turn on GoGoPi ​

  1. Switch the battery box on, and turn on the switch on the Raspberry Pi expansion board. The LED1 and LED2 of the Raspberry Pi will be on firstly, then LED2 will flash every 1 second, the pan-tilt will return to the initial position, and the buzzer will make a “beep” sound. This indicates that the robot is turned on successfully.

The Raspberry Pi itself is also a computer, and it takes a short time to start. The startup time is about 30 seconds, so please be patient.

After turning on, Raspberry Pi will be in a AP hotspot mode and launch a Wi-Fi hotspot named with the first letters “HW”.
